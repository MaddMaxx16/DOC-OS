## Unload Sequencing Onboarding Polish

AR1 keeps the successful AR unload sequencing mechanic intact and clarifies the first experience.

- First unload challenge in the app session opens an explicit `UNLOAD IN ORDER` briefing.
- Briefing teaches three rules: rear-accessible pallets move, two staging spaces clear blockers, requested freight goes to the receiver.
- Adds a simple `TRAILER -> STAGING -> RECEIVER` visual flow cue.
- Player explicitly taps `GOT IT · START UNLOADING`; the dock timer and puzzle interaction remain paused until the briefing clears.
- Accessible trailer pallets and open staging spaces receive temporary first-move emphasis.
- Guidance disappears after the player's first successful move.
- Repeat unload challenges use a short `UNLOAD SEQUENCE` title sting instead of replaying the full tutorial.
- No AR sequencing, freight-truth, move scoring, delay, POD, pickup, or AQ delivery-lifecycle rules changed.
